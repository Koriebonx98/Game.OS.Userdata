KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

HOW TO PLAY.

Answer fast, answer right.

01 / SUBJECTS

PICK 3 TO 5

Each player chooses 3 to 5 subjects. Questions are randomised from everyone's choices, so no two games are the same.

02 / ANSWERING

A, B, C OR D

Tap an answer or press A–D (or 1–4). Faster correct answers score more, and streaks add bonus points.

03 / SPELLING

LISTEN AND SPELL

The word is read out loud. Type it exactly: spelling counts, and auto-correct is switched off. Tap Hear again as often as you like.

04 / ROUNDS

PUB QUIZ: THREE ROUNDS

Mixed Subjects (10 questions), Picture Round (name the place or celebrity from the photo), then Quick Fire (6 seconds each).

05 / PUB QUIZ

THE DEFAULT

Three rounds and the most points wins. This is how Quizy always played, and it is what you get unless the host picks another mode.

06 / THE CHASE

OUTRUN THE CHASER

Everyone builds cash with five quick questions (£1,000 each). Then each player picks an offer (higher pays more but starts closer to the Chaser) and races down the board: a right answer moves you down a step, and the Chaser moves on every right answer of their own. Reach home, or get caught. Survivors team up for a final chase: beat the Chaser's score and share the prize.

07 / MILLIONAIRE

FIFTEEN TO A MILLION

Everyone climbs their own ladder on the same questions. A wrong answer drops you to the last safe haven (question 5 or 10) and you're out, or walk away to keep what you have. Each player gets 50:50, Ask the Audience and Phone a Friend once.

08 / ONLINE

FRIENDS OR ANYONE

Invite Game OS friends, or tick Public so anyone signed in can join, or use Online Match to hop into a public lobby straight away.

09 / WEEKLY

NEW QUESTIONS EVERY WEEK

Fresh questions are added every Monday, and the maths and spelling questions rotate weekly too. After your first visit Quizy plays offline, picture rounds need a connection for the photos.
